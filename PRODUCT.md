# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Current and prospective Informatics and Electronics Engineering students at International Hellenic University who want to meet the IEESEC team, understand its work, or apply to join.

## Product Purpose

Present IEESEC as an active software engineering community, showcase the people and technologies behind it, and give interested students a clear path into the team.

## Brand Personality

Practical, collaborative, and technically confident. The voice should feel like capable students building together, not a corporate software vendor or an institutional prospectus.

## Anti-references

- Do not redesign the established campus-led visual concept.
- Avoid translucent haze, washed-out text, decorative glow, and surfaces that disappear into the page.
- Avoid generic corporate technology styling or overly formal university branding.

## Design Principles

- Show the real campus and the real team.
- Make technical confidence approachable.
- Preserve clarity while keeping the interface expressive.
- Use the steel-blue identity deliberately and consistently.
- Keep joining the community straightforward and trustworthy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast requirements in both themes. Keep visible keyboard focus, respect reduced-motion preferences, and never rely on color alone to communicate state.
