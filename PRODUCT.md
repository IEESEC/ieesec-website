# Product direction

## Product

The IEESEC website is the public entry point for the IEESEC Software Engineering student community at the International Hellenic University. It explains who the community is, shows its people and technical interests, and gives prospective members a trustworthy way to apply.

## Audience

- Current and prospective students of the Department of Information and Electronic Engineering.
- Students considering participation in projects, workshops or events.
- University staff, collaborators and organisations evaluating the community's work.
- IEESEC members who need a stable public reference for the community.

## Outcomes

A visitor should be able to:

1. Understand what IEESEC is and where it is based.
2. Recognise the people and technologies behind the community.
3. Find community contact and Discord links.
4. Apply to join in Greek or English with clear privacy expectations.
5. Use the site with keyboard navigation, reduced motion or a compact device.

## Brand personality

Practical, collaborative and technically confident. The voice should feel like capable students building together, not a corporate software vendor or an institutional prospectus.

## Content ownership

| Content                                   | Accountable role                              |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Product claims and public contact details | Community chair                               |
| Member profiles and consent               | Community chair plus the represented member   |
| Projects and technical claims             | Relevant project owner                        |
| Events and dates                          | Event organiser                               |
| Join-form questions and processing        | Application coordinator                       |
| Privacy notice and retention practice     | Community chair with appropriate legal review |
| Design system and accessibility           | Frontend maintainers                          |

Every owner reviews changes through a pull request. Content update procedures are in [docs/content-guide.md](docs/content-guide.md).

## Product principles

- Show the real campus, community and work.
- Make technical confidence approachable.
- Keep joining straightforward and trustworthy.
- Prefer accurate, maintained content over decorative placeholders.
- Treat performance, accessibility, privacy and security as product requirements.
- Preserve a complete Greek and English experience.

## Anti-references

- Do not redesign the established campus-led visual concept without an explicit product decision.
- Avoid translucent haze, washed-out text, decorative glow and surfaces that disappear into the page.
- Avoid generic corporate technology styling or overly formal university branding.
- Do not publish invented projects, events, articles, metrics or member claims.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast requirements in both themes. Keep visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks and meaningful labels, respect reduced-motion preferences, and never rely on colour alone to communicate state.

## Product-change checklist

- Does this help one of the defined audiences complete a real task?
- Is the capability active, experimental or placeholder, and is that status honest?
- Is ownership clear after release?
- Are Greek and English experiences complete?
- Have accessibility, privacy, security and operational effects been reviewed?
- Are tests, content guidance and runbooks updated?
